On-Campus Accommodation

🏠 Halls of Residence (Female)

Located on the east side of campus behind the Library. Access is restricted to female students only. Has a common room, laundry area, and power backup.

~11 mins from gate Female only

🏠 Halls of Residence (Male)

Male accommodation blocks adjacent to the female halls. Known for tight security and an on-site generator. Room allocation is competitive — apply early each session.

~11 mins from gate Male only

🏠 Postgraduate Hostel

Reserved for postgraduate and masters students. Self-contained rooms available. Apply through the Bursary unit at the Senate Building.

~12 mins from gate PG Students

🏘️ Off-Campus Housing Tips

Most LASU students live off-campus in Ojo, Igbo Elerin, Alaba Rago, or Trade Fair areas. Closest and cheapest is along the Ojo road corridor. Shared apartments for ₦80k–₦200k/yr are common for CS students.
